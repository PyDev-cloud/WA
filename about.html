Who We Are

Dune Tech IT is a locally operated IT support company based in Perth, providing reliable and affordable technology solutions for both residential and business clients across Western Australia.

Our experienced and certified technicians specialise in computer and laptop repairs, network setup, cloud solutions and ongoing IT support. We work with home users, small businesses and growing organisations that need dependable IT services they can trust.

Our Experience & Expertise

With years of hands-on industry experience, our Perth IT professionals focus on delivering long-term solutions — not temporary fixes. We stay up to date with modern tools, security practices and industry standards to ensure our clients remain protected and productive.

Local IT Support You Can Trust

Being based in Shelley, WA allows us to provide fast on-site IT support throughout Perth and surrounding suburbs including Cannington, Victoria Park, Fremantle and nearby areas.

We believe in transparent pricing, honest advice and friendly customer service. Our goal is to build long-term relationships by delivering consistent, high-quality IT support across Western Australia.
